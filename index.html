How I Work

Engineering principles.

The ideas I keep coming back to when the complexity goes up.

6 principles
01boundaries · ownership

"Architecture is a product feature."

Module boundaries and data ownership decide whether the next ten features ship in two weeks or two quarters. I design for the second-year codebase, not the demo.

02schema-first · codegen

"Type the contract, not the screen."

TypeScript pays for itself when types describe the API, not just the props. Generated clients and discriminated unions catch the bugs that would otherwise hit a tenant in production.

03RUM · budgets · p95

"Observability before optimisation."

I instrument before I tune. Real User Monitoring and render budgets tell me what to fix. Performance is a number, not a vibe.

04tokens · primitives

"Design systems are graphs, not folders."

A component library only compounds when tokens, primitives and patterns describe relationships — light/dark, density, tenant brand, motion. Systems that survive a redesign without a rewrite.

05DAU > demo

"Optimise for the analyst, not the demo."

Power users live inside the product 8 hours a day. Keyboard-first, bulk-action, undo-everything. Density beats whitespace when the user is paid to be fast.

06flags · canary · audit

"Ship the rollback first."

Feature flags, canary tenants, audit logs and a clean back-out path before the feature lands. Boring? Yes. That's how production frontend teams keep their nights.
